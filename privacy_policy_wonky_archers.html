Privacy Policy – Wonky Archers: Stickman Duel

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Developer: Florek Games (Jakub Florkowski)

Short version: the game has no accounts and stores your progress only on your device. To keep the game free we show ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

Data the game itself stores

Progress (ladder progress, coins, bows, arrows and hats, best scores, daily duel streak, settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your consent and your device settings, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers (advertising ID / IDFA, app set ID),
  • approximate location derived from the IP address,
  • app interaction and diagnostic data (for example ad views, crashes, performance).

This is used to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, we ask for your choice with Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the game. On iOS, personalised ads also require your App Tracking Transparency permission.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · AdMob and your privacy

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which modes people play and to fix problems, the app sends anonymous game events (for example "duel started", "duel finished", ad shown) to Google Analytics for Firebase. These events are tied to a random app instance ID, not to your name or account, and are not used to track you across other companies' apps. Collection starts only after the consent message above; if you decline, analytics consent is set to denied.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
